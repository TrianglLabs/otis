Otis's source code is released under the [MIT License](LICENSE). That license covers the code only. It grants no
rights to Triangl Labs, Inc.'s names, logos, icons, or other branding.

## What Triangl Labs, Inc. claims

Triangl Labs, Inc. claims the following as its marks and brand assets, registered or not:

- **Otis** as the name of this software, and **Triangl Labs**
- The Otis logo, the cube mark, and the application icons, including the files in

- The look of the Otis apps and website as a whole
- The official domain `triangllabs.ai`, and the official repository, releases, and install scripts listed below

## Forks and derived projects

You may fork, change, build, and redistribute the code under the MIT License. If you publish a fork, a custom build,
a package, a website, or any other project derived from Otis:

- **Brand it as your own.** Replace the Otis name, logo, cube mark, and application icons with your own, in the
  app, its installers, its update feed, its listings, and its website.
- **Say clearly that it is unofficial.** State plainly, where people will see it, that your project is not affiliated
  with, endorsed by, or maintained by Triangl Labs, Inc.
- **Keep credit to plain facts.** You may say in plain text that your project is based on the Otis source code, for
  example "An unofficial fork of Otis". Do not use the Otis logo, cube mark, or icons to do it, and do not use "Otis"
  as your project's name, app name, package name, or domain.

## What is not allowed

Do not use the Otis name, logo, cube mark, icons, or look in a way that suggests your project is official, or that
Triangl Labs, Inc. makes, sponsors, endorses, or supports it. That includes builds that keep the Otis name or icon,
installers or update feeds that look like ours, and listings or domains that could be mistaken for ours.

Triangl Labs, Inc. enforces these rights. Projects that present themselves as official Otis, or impersonate
Triangl Labs, Inc., will be reported to the platforms that host them, challenged over confusing domains, and pursued
legally where needed.
